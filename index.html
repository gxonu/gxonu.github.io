About

I am a senior undergraduate student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Inha University, currently working as a research intern at DAVIAN Lab, Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI, KAIST, advised by Professor Jaegul Choo.

My research interests lie in generative models, multimodal learning, and computer vision. I am currently working on 3D geometry-aware multi-view subject-to-video generation, with a co-first-author paper in preparation. Previously, I studied zero-shot subject-driven image editing with diffusion models at the Multimodal AI Lab, Inha University.

I believe generative AI should understand the context and structure of the world — and that there is no failure, only growth.

Research Highlights

MV3D-S2V — 3D Geometry-aware Multi-view Subject-to-Video Generation

2026 — ongoing

DAVIAN Lab, KAIST · co-first author, paper in preparation

Supplies 3D geometry — rather than assuming it — for subject-to-video generation: a 3D-consistent data synthesis pipeline plus geometry-aware conditioning routes per-frame view evidence from reconstructed 3D structure, preserving subject identity from every angle without per-subject optimization or dozens of references.

Zero-shot Subject-Driven Image Editing

2024 — 2025

Multimodal AI Lab, Inha University

Training-free personalization of image editing under limited compute. Identified the structural limits of bbox conditioning and the identity–background trade-off; the insight that identity preservation comes from understanding relations between conditions — not more training — led directly to the current video generation work.
