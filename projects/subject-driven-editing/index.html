Research reproduction / 2024–2025

Training-Free Subject-Driven Image Editing

Undergraduate Researcher, Inha University

Reimplementation and extension of subject-driven image editing with diffusion models, including partial-region editing.

Code

Research context

Studied subject-driven image editing at the Multimodal AI Lab, Inha University, from December 2024 to December 2025.

Work

Reproduced and extended training-free editing methods and explored how conditioning and region selection affect identity preservation and background changes.

Attribution

This is a reproduction and extension project. The upstream Diptych Prompting paper belongs to its original authors and is not listed as my publication. The repository describes the upstream method and local implementation.
