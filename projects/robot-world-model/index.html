Action-Conditioned Robot World Model

Team leader

Predicting future video from an image and a robot action sequence, with action-conditioned adaptation and inverse-dynamics consistency.

Top Excellence Award, graduate track (2nd place)

CodeSlidesCertificate

Problem

Generate 16 future frames from one observation and a sequence of five joint target positions plus a gripper value. The generated motion needs to follow the requested action as well as look plausible.

Approach

Led the project from task analysis to final submission. Adapted Cosmos-Predict2.5 with LoRA and an action embedder, used two-stage training, and introduced an inverse-dynamics consistency objective to connect the generated motion to the action input.

Evaluation

The final Private score was 0.19226 (lower is better), earning 2nd place in the graduate track and the Top Excellence Award.

Resources

The repository contains training and inference code, experiment notes, presentation slides and qualitative comparisons. Model weights are not currently included in the public release.
