AI product / 2025

COSFLOW at UPFLOW

Co-founding Member / AI Researcher

Regulation search and request-form automation for cosmetic OEM teams, using MCP collection and retrieval-grounded reports.

Website

Problem

Cosmetics manufacturing teams repeatedly search regulatory sources across countries and transfer unstructured customer requests into standard forms.

Work

Built MCP-based collection across five countries and a LangChain RAG engine that generates reports with supporting sources. Compared five language models on real work scenarios and used structured outputs to produce standard request forms.

Evaluation

Tested complex ingredient names and negative constraints such as “free from” requirements, then organized recognition errors into improvement tasks. The company progressed to a paid pilot.

Experience

Co-founding member and AI researcher at UPFLOW from February to October 2025.
