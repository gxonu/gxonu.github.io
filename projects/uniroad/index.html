Co-founding Member / AI Researcher

An admissions counseling service that connects structured admissions data and student context to answers with source citations.

Service

Context

University admissions decisions depend on institution-specific documents and a student’s individual record. The service retrieves relevant source material rather than relying only on a language model’s stored knowledge.

Work

As a co-founding member and AI researcher from March to July 2026, designed the admissions data structure and Agentic RAG engine. Developed contextual answers from uploaded student records and a workflow that cites original sources and abstains on low-confidence questions.

Product operations

Built automated community answers and short-form content for organic acquisition. Continued providing external support after July 2026.
